# 🎓 QS Nexus

> A modern academic portal for the Department of Quantity Surveying, University of Ibadan.

---

## 📖 Overview

QS Nexus is a digital departmental portal designed to improve communication and academic resource management within the Department of Quantity Surveying.

The platform connects:

- 👨‍🎓 Students
- 👨‍🏫 Lecturers
- 👑 Head of Department (HOD)
- 🏢 Department Administrators

---

## ✨ Features

### Public Portal

- Home Page
- About Department
- Course Catalogue
- Resources
- Department Announcements
- Contact Information

### Student Portal

- Secure Login
- Dashboard
- Course Materials
- Past Questions
- Assignment Submission
- Notifications
- Academic Profile

### Lecturer Portal

- Secure Login
- Upload Lecture Slides
- Upload Assignments
- Upload Past Questions
- Post Announcements
- Manage Courses

### HOD/Admin Portal

- Dashboard
- User Management
- Course Management
- Department Announcements
- Analytics
