# QS Nexus

## Project Overview

QS Nexus is a modern departmental portal for the Department of Quantity Surveying, University of Ibadan.

The system is designed for:

- Students (100L–500L)
- Lecturers
- HOD
- Department Administrators

The portal will provide academic resources, communication tools, and departmental management features.
